Malaysia's stablecoin plans, Stripe's agent payments, Claude Opus 4.6 migrations, and Alibaba's Solana RPC demo.

Malaysia’s Central Bank Launches Three Stablecoin Initiatives

Malaysia’s central bank to launch three stablecoin and tokenized deposit initiatives in 2026

Bank Negara Malaysia is rolling out three separate stablecoin and tokenized deposit programs this year. This isn’t a pilot—it’s a coordinated policy push from a major Asian economy. If successful, it sets a template for regulated stablecoin frameworks across Southeast Asia.

Stripe Integrates x402 for USDC Agent Payments on Base

Stripe adds x402 integration for USDC agent payments on Base

Stripe now supports x402 protocol for autonomous agent payments in USDC on Base. AI agents can now pay for services programmatically without human intervention. This is the plumbing for the agent economy—automated workflows that settle in real-time with stablecoins.

Claude Opus 4.6 Migrates 456-Page Site in One Shot

X: drew.sh on WordPress → Jekyll migration

Developer Drew Cook watched Claude Opus 4.6 migrate his entire 456-page WordPress site to Jekyll in a single run. No incremental prompting, no manual cleanup—just done. This is the inflection point where AI crosses from “helpful assistant” to “replaces entire workflows.”

Alibaba Demos High-Performance Solana RPCs

X: Solana on Alibaba RPC demo

Alibaba, the world’s largest ecommerce company, just demoed high-performance Solana RPC infrastructure. This isn’t Solana selling to Alibaba—it’s Alibaba building on Solana. When trillion-dollar web2 giants choose your chain for production infrastructure, that’s validation beyond any VC tweet.

Franklin Templeton + Binance: Tokenized Money Funds as Trading Collateral

Franklin Templeton, Binance roll out program letting institutions use tokenized money funds as trading collateral

TradFi asset manager Franklin Templeton partnered with Binance to let institutions use tokenized money market funds as collateral for crypto trading. This closes the loop: institutions can deploy capital without selling treasuries, maintaining yield while accessing leverage. RWAs just became functional, not theoretical.

Bitcoin Slides Below $67K on Hawkish Macro Outlook

Bitcoin slides below $67,000 as traders digest hawkish US macro outlook

BTC dropped below $67K as traders process signals that the Fed won’t pivot as fast as hoped. The correlation between rate expectations and crypto prices remains tight. We’re not decoupled—we’re just earlier in the risk curve.

The Rise of “Vibe Coding”

X: Lenny Rachitsky on professional vibe coders

Lenny Rachitsky’s interview with Lazar Jovanovic (@lakikentaki), who gets paid full-time to “vibe code” with AI tools. His take: “Should I start learning to code? I’d honestly say no. You’re optimizing for the wrong skillset.” Product sense + AI tooling beats traditional dev skills for entire classes of work. This is the new career path opening up.
