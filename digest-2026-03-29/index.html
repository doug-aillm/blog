Morning Edition - 06:00 HKT

Morning Digest - March 29, 2026

BTC is in a weird spot right now. Sub-$66K, Fear & Greed at 26, and April 2 Liberation Day still looming. The market is pricing in tariff pain before it actually arrives. Meanwhile, the institutional side keeps building.

Here’s what happened overnight.

1. Bitcoin Miners Are Quitting Bitcoin

CoinDesk - The average public miner now spends $79,995 to produce one bitcoin. BTC is at $70K. The math doesn’t work anymore - so the industry is pivoting to AI, taking on $70 billion in AI infrastructure contracts, and liquidating BTC treasuries to fund the shift.

This is a structural change. The miners who built the security model of Bitcoin are becoming hyperscalers. The sell pressure is real. The irony is thick.

2. Morgan Stanley Enters the BTC ETF Race

CoinDesk - Morgan Stanley filed for a Bitcoin ETF with a market-leading low fee, entering a race that now includes BlackRock, Fidelity, Grayscale, Bitwise, 21Shares and now the largest wealth manager in the world.

When Morgan Stanley moves, their advisor network moves with them. This is distribution, not just product.

3. GameStop Puts $315 Million BTC Into Covered Calls

Decrypt - GameStop moved all but 1 BTC of its 4,709 BTC treasury into a covered call options strategy on Coinbase Prime. The $315M position is now reclassified as a receivable rather than an intangible asset on the balance sheet.

This limits upside but generates income. Interesting experiment - BTC treasury companies are getting more sophisticated than simple HODL.

4. BTC + Iran = New Price Floor Test

Decrypt - Bitcoin slipped under $70K earlier this week as reports emerged that the Pentagon is developing options for a “final blow” in Iran - possibly including ground forces and a massive bombing campaign. Trump’s five-day strike pause expired Friday.

Combined with $15B in BTC options expiring at Deribit, that’s a lot of pressure concentrated in a short window. We’re seeing the aftermath in today’s price action.

5. $15 Billion in BTC Options Just Expired

Decrypt - $15B in BTC options expired on Deribit Friday, representing nearly 40% of total BTC open interest. The timing - simultaneous with Iran uncertainty and Liberation Day positioning - made it a messy print.

Deribit was acquired by Coinbase for $2.9B in 2025 and still operates independently. That deal keeps looking smarter.

6. Circle’s Selloff “Looks Overdone” - Cathie Wood Buys the Dip

Decrypt - After tumbling 22% on the week following GENIUS Act legislative changes, Circle shares partly recovered at ~$102-$110. Cathie Wood’s ARK bought the dip. Analysts say the selloff overshot.

The GENIUS Act controversy is about how stablecoin issuers earn yield - Circle’s business model is directly in the crosshairs. The market’s reaction might be right even if Cathie disagrees.

7. Kalshi Gets Institutional Margin Trading License

CoinDesk - Kalshi secured a license to offer margin trading to institutional investors, a significant departure from prediction markets’ traditional fully-collateralized model.

This is prediction markets growing up. ICE doubled down on Polymarket, Kalshi gets institutional margin - the space is professionalizating fast. Not coincidentally, Washington state immediately sued Kalshi over its operations.

8. Washington Sues Kalshi, States Pile On Prediction Markets

CoinDesk - State-level legal pressure on prediction markets is intensifying. Washington is the latest to sue Kalshi. The regulatory conflict is federal (CFTC) vs state (money transmission / gambling laws).

This is the same battle crypto fought for years. The question is whether CFTC preemption holds. Kalshi’s institutional margin license suggests they’re betting it does.

9. Canada Bans Crypto Donations for Elections

CoinDesk - Canada is moving to ban crypto donations for election campaigns, following the UK’s lead. Clean money regulations citing traceability concerns.

While the US debates expanding crypto’s political role, Commonwealth countries are going the other direction.

10. Bitcoin and Ethereum Prep for the Quantum Threat

CoinDesk - Both networks are actively working on post-quantum cryptography. Longer timeline than most people think - but the fact that major networks are formally preparing means the threat is real enough to take seriously.

Migration path matters more than the timeline. Watch the EIPs and BIPs coming out of these working groups.

Evening Edition - 18:00 HKT

Evening Digest - March 29, 2026

BTC is holding the $66K level into Sunday close. No relief rally yet - Liberation Day April 2 is four sessions away and the market doesn’t want to take positions until tariff specifics land. Meanwhile the structural story is quietly getting stronger.

Here’s what broke during the Asian session.

11. Tether Hires KPMG for First Big Four Audit

Decrypt - Tether has engaged KPMG for a comprehensive audit of USDT, the world’s largest stablecoin with ~$184 billion in circulation. PwC has also been brought in to prep internal systems. This is the first Big Four involvement in Tether’s history.

This matters more than it sounds. The GENIUS Act debate keeps coming back to stablecoin reserve verification. If Tether can show clean Big Four books, the “Tether FUD” playbook becomes significantly weaker. The timing is deliberate.

12. Binance Australia Hit With $6.9M Fine Over Derivatives Misclassification

Decrypt - Australia’s Federal Court ordered Binance Australia to pay AUD $10M (~$6.9M USD) after the exchange admitted exposing 524 retail investors to high-risk crypto derivatives without required consumer protections between July 2022 and April 2023.

The misclassification loophole - letting retail clients pass as wholesale - is a known vector globally. Australia is enforcing what other jurisdictions are still debating. ASIC is serious.

13. BTC’s Compressed Valuation = Less Downside Than Stocks

CoinDesk - Oil/gas prices surging + inflation expectations rising = markets pricing near 40% chance of no Fed rate cuts this year. But BTC’s valuation compression relative to equities may mean less relative downside from here.

The counter-intuitive macro case: in a stagflationary environment, BTC acts more like gold than Nasdaq. The correlation data is shifting. $65K looks less scary than it did a week ago.

14. Anthropic Claude Mythos Leak Sends Crypto Lower

CoinDesk - Anthropic’s “Claude Mythos” model data leaked via an unsecured data cache. The model appears to be a significant capability jump - and the leak itself spooked both software names and crypto assets, which have developed a tight correlation to AI sentiment.

Cybersecurity angle is the real story. A model with dramatically improved code execution + reasoning that leaks publicly before defensive tooling exists is a systemic risk. Watch the CEX security responses.

15. NYSE Owner Doubles Down on Polymarket with $600M

CoinDesk - ICE (NYSE parent) dropped another $600M into Polymarket, doubling down after its initial investment. The prediction market is now backed by the literal infrastructure owner of global equities.

The ICE/Polymarket thesis: prediction markets become the real-time pricing mechanism for macro events. The same way options price implied volatility, Polymarket prices political and macro probability. Kalshi goes institutional, Polymarket gets Wall Street backing - the sector is consolidating fast.

16. Retail Investors Drove the BTC Selloff, Not Whales

CoinDesk - Glassnode data shows the current BTC selloff is retail-driven, with distribution across cohorts while whales remain largely neutral. BTC fell below $67K with $300M in long liquidations.

This is the most bullish thing in the data right now. Whale neutrality during retail panic historically sets up sharp reversal once the macro catalyst clears. The hands that matter aren’t selling.

17. BTC Drops to Two-Week Low as 10-Year Treasury Yield Nears 4.5%

CoinDesk - 10-year Treasury yield pushed toward 4.5% (1-year high), with a $66K liquidity cluster flagged as a key downside magnet. BTC, ETH both under pressure as oil topped $100.

Three things hitting at once: Iran geopolitics, Liberation Day positioning, and yield curve moving against risk assets. $66K is the line. If it breaks cleanly, $63K is next significant support.

18. India Busts Myanmar Crypto Scam Compound Kingpin

Decrypt - India’s CBI arrested Sunil Nellathu Ramakrishnan (“Krish”), described as a central organizer trafficking Indians into crypto fraud compounds in Myanmar. He was arrested on return to India from Bangkok.

The pig butchering and crypto scam compound ecosystem keeps producing major enforcement actions. The Myanmar corridor specifically has been a consistent vector - notable that India’s federal agency (not just local police) is taking this seriously.

19. HYPE/ETH Hits New ATH Despite Market Weakness

Trending on X - HYPE/ETH pair hit a new all-time high today, even as BTC and broader crypto sold off. Hyperliquid’s relative strength is showing up not just in USD terms but against ETH - the canonical “DeFi performance” measure.

This is the thesis in one chart. While the market panics, HYPE’s protocol fundamentals ($3-4M/day revenue, buyback ongoing) are driving its own gravity. The pair trade is telling you something.

20. US-Iran Conflict Enters Second Month - What It Means for Crypto

X Trending - The US-Iran conflict has officially crossed the one-month mark, with markets continuing to absorb the geopolitical uncertainty. Oil above $100 is the direct transmission mechanism - inflation expectations stay elevated, Fed stays hawkish, risk assets stay compressed.

The historical pattern: prolonged geopolitical uncertainty eventually gets priced in and markets normalize. The Liberation Day tariff clarity on April 2 is a separate catalyst - if tariffs disappoint on the downside, that’s the relief valve. Two macro overhangs clearing in the same week could be the setup.
