2. Solana Quantum Security Test Results: Speed Costs a Lot

Solana’s security team published quantum resistance benchmarks overnight. The tradeoff is stark: post-quantum signature schemes add 2-10x computational overhead per transaction. At 65,000 TPS, that math doesn’t work without hardware changes. It’s an honest admission that “quantum-resistant” and “high-throughput L1” aren’t free at the same time - you have to choose which to prioritize first. The migration path will matter more than the destination.

2. AI Agents Are Already Paying With Stablecoins

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong’s prediction - stablecoin transactions will grow 100x as AI agents outnumber humans - looks obvious once you understand x402. The protocol works like this: an AI agent makes an HTTP request, gets a 402 Payment Required response, pays a stablecoin micropayment, then gets the resource. Claude Code already has it embedded. Every API call from an AI agent becomes an autonomous stablecoin payment. The 100x volume prediction is probably underselling it.

Trending | Technology / AI Agents

3. Central Banks Are Bringing Repo Markets Onchain

UBS. Société Générale. Banque de France. Not experimenting - running actual repo transactions on Ethereum. The global repo market is $12.5 trillion. Even 1% onchain is $125 billion. The institutions that spent two years saying “blockchain not Bitcoin” are now deploying real capital on public infrastructure. This is what Bryan Pellegrino meant when he said sentiment “flipped.”

11,885 views | @CryptoTice_

4. Solana Quantum Test: The Numbers Are Brutal

The story ran in the morning but the data is worth sitting with. Post-quantum signature schemes (CRYSTALS-Dilithium, FALCON) produce signatures 40x larger than Ed25519. On Solana at 65,000 TPS, the signature bandwidth alone breaks the throughput model. The test result: 90% speed reduction. Every high-throughput L1 faces this same wall - Solana just ran the benchmark honestly. The migration path will matter more than picking the right PQ algorithm.

1,232 posts | Project Eleven data

5. Coinbase OCC Charter: Infrastructure, Not Headlines

At 40,300 posts and still climbing, the Coinbase federal bank charter story keeps finding new angles. The one worth focusing on: a national trust bank charter means Coinbase operates under a single federal regulator instead of maintaining 50 separate state money transmitter licenses. A national charter is a structural change - it reshapes the unit economics of institutional custody. More institutional custody capacity at lower compliance cost equals more institutional clients. Community bank opposition is noise until a legal challenge materializes.
